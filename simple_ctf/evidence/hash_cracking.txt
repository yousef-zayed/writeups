Attempt 3 - hashes.com precomputed lookup:
0c01f4468bd75d7a84c7eb73846e8d96:1dac0d92e9fa6bb2secret

Analysis:
The hash is salted (CMSMS 2.x hashes as MD5(salt + password)), which is
why a plain md5("secret") = 5ebe2294ecd0e0f08eab7690d2a6ee69 didn't
match it directly, and why a straight rockyou dictionary attack against
the raw hash failed - the salt "1dac0d92e9fa6bb2" isn't in the
wordlist, and neither tool was given the salt separately to combine
with candidates.

Recovered password: secret
